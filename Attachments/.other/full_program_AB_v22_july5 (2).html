Active Recovery — Session G4 (Version A) · ≤40 min HARD CAP · precedes intervals
Purpose: prime movement + blood flow for tomorrow's intervals — NOT accumulate volume. 2 sets max, RPE 5–6, leave 3–4 reps in reserve (RIR 3–4), HR <115. No overload attempts, no failure, no extra sets or rounds. Legs stay minimal so they are fresh for intervals. Timer ≤40 min, then stop. Deload Week — every 4–6 weeks (align with the running cutback week)
Why: the recurring set-3 / round-4 drops (Smith bench, chin-up, T-bar row) are accumulated-fatigue markers, not strength ceilings — a planned deload lets them rebound. Your running already cuts back every 4th long run; run the gym deload in the same week for one systemic recovery week. Next: week of Sat Jul 18 (R37 cutback 12 km).
